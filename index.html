Signal ↔ process

Type in any one box — the rest follow. With a linear transfer function the two percentages are equal; with square root they are not, which is the point of the setting.

Error at this point

The ideal signal is what the transmitter should produce for the process value above, so the error is the difference between that and what you actually read. % of reading grows without limit near the bottom of the range — below about 10 % of span it is rarely a meaningful way to state error, which is why most transmitter specifications are written as % of span.

Hysteresis is the largest up/down difference at the same point. Linearity here is the largest deviation from the ideal straight line across the as-left up run. Repeatability needs repeat runs and is not derived from a single up/down pair.

Loop compliance & burden

Maximum load = (supply − transmitter minimum) ÷ maximum loop current. Sizing at 20 mA alone leaves no headroom for over-range or alarm currents, so the default checks 22 mA.
